# Roadmap

1. **Data** — list of in-scope attack items, read from every collectible page on the wiki and
   reviewed by hand.
2. **General rules** — weapons (`replaces`), effects (`adds`) and shot multipliers (`multiplies`)
   extracted from the wiki, each with its source.
3. **Prototype** — item picker, interactions panel and a simple animated preview for about ten
   items (Brimstone, Mom's Knife, Ipecac, Tech X, 20/20, ...).
4. **First community feedback** — share the prototype (r/bindingofisaac, wiki Discord).
5. **Expansion** — all in-scope items, then animated `exception` interactions, most common first.
6. **Release** — stable site on GitHub Pages, with a periodic re-sync from the wiki.
